# 01 — Discovery

> Reconstructed from my project notes. The prompt is summarized, not verbatim.

## Goal
Frame the problem and the users before deciding what to build.

## Prompt
*Summary:* Guests keep asking the same things by message at every stay. Help me design something for the cabin's guests that answers those questions before they need to ask.

## What came back
Not recorded in detail. The outcome is the product definition below.

## What I kept, changed or rejected
- **Kept — a curated guide, not a Q&A tool.** Guests need a small set of basic answers at the right moment, not a conversation. A guide is faster to read on a phone, has no running cost and cannot give a wrong answer.
- **Kept — users mapped by moment of the stay.** Before arrival, during the stay and while exploring the region, plus the host, who wants one source of truth to send at every booking (see the users table in the main README).
- **On the roadmap — an AI assistant grounded only in the guide's content**, with handoff to the host, as a possible next step once the guide is stable.

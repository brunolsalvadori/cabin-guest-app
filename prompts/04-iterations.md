# 04 — Iterations

> Part 1 is reconstructed from my project notes. Part 2 quotes the actual decision, translated from Portuguese.

## Part 1 — Which guide opens first

### Goal
Decide what a guest sees when they open the link.

### Prompt
*Summary:* Put the hospitality guide on the landing page and link the tourist guide from it as one icon.

### What came back
The restructured site: hospitality guide at the root, tourist guide as one of its icons.

### What I kept, changed or rejected
- **Kept — the hospitality guide as the landing page.** Guests open the link to solve a stay problem (arriving, checking in, getting the hot tub going). Tourism is a secondary job, one icon away.

## Part 2 — How to protect access information

### Goal
Make sure a forwarded link cannot be used to enter the property.

### Prompt
A review of the live site found it was not password protected, although the original plan was a password-protected site. Two options were put to me: turn on password protection (a paid hosting feature) or keep every access code out of the site. My answer:

> "Take the codes out of the site."

### What came back
A check of every page showed the lockbox and door codes were already not on the site: the access page shows only the how-to videos and says the codes arrive in the platform's welcome message.

### What I kept, changed or rejected
- **Kept — codes travel only in the platform welcome message**, never on the site. A forwarded link reveals nothing that opens the property, at no cost and with no password for guests to manage.
- **Next — apply the same rule to the Wi-Fi password** and update the footer note, which still says the guide contains access codes.

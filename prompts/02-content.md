# 02 — Content

> Reconstructed from my project notes and the production guide. The prompt is summarized, not verbatim.

## Goal
Select the essential and most-asked information, and nothing more.

## Prompt
*Summary:* From the questions guests ask most and how the cabin works, propose the sections of the guide and what each one must say.

## What came back
Not recorded in detail. The sections below are what went live.

## What I kept, changed or rejected
- **Kept — 14 short sections, each answering one question:** town guide, check-in and check-out, keys and access, directions, Wi-Fi, hot tub, fireplace, firewood and fire pit, outdoor lights, welcome coffee, house rules, emergency, before you leave, and contact.
- **Kept — video for anything with steps.** Opening the gate lockbox and the door, using the hot tub and lighting the fireplace are each a short video, because guests follow a demonstration more easily than written steps.
- **Kept — the tourist guide as its own page.** Places are grouped by category (restaurants, olive groves, vineyard and coffee farm, artisanal cheese, cafés and bakeries, waterfalls, sights, crafts, practical services), each with hours, a route from the cabin, a map link and WhatsApp.
- **Kept — emergency numbers that work from any phone**, free of charge and available 24 hours.
